# Country photographs

Drop aerial/landscape photos here to replace the painted-SVG fallbacks on
country cards. Expected filenames (match the country code):

- br.jpg, Brazil  (Amazon canopy, misty forest)
- pe.jpg, Peru    (Andean peaks, Sacred Valley, Amazon basin)
- in.jpg, India   (monsoon forest, tea terraces)
- cn.jpg, China   (mountain mist, Huangshan)
- mx.jpg, Mexico  (Chihuahuan desert, Yucatán)
- ke.jpg, Kenya   (Maasai Mara savanna, acacia at sunset)
- au.jpg, Australia (outback red earth, eucalyptus)
- ma.jpg, Morocco (Atlas range, argan grove)
- id.jpg, Indonesia (rice terraces, Bali jungle)
- jp.jpg, Japan   (satoyama, misty Kyoto hills)
- za.jpg, South Africa (Cederberg, fynbos)
- jm.jpg, Jamaica (Blue Mountains, bush)

Recommended: landscape 1600×1000+ JPG, around 200–500KB. The cards apply a
dark gradient overlay, so medium-to-rich saturation works best.
